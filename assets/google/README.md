# Sign in with Google button

`sign-in-with-google-pill.png` is the selected sign-in button: Google's unmodified
pre-approved Android/Web light pill button at 2× resolution (360 × 80).
`sign-in-with-google.png` is the rectangular alternative. Both are downloaded from
https://developers.google.com/static/identity/images/signin-assets.zip.

Use the native aspect ratio with no filters, shadow, recoloring, or replacement
lettering. Give its link an accessible name. This branding asset is provided by
Google for its sign-in flow; it is not part of Infuser's original logo artwork.

Guidelines: https://developers.google.com/identity/branding-guidelines
