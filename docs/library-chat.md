# Chat and phone notifications

Audience: players and admins.

Open **Chat** beside the online players at the top of the Library. Everyone in
your private group shares the room. Enter sends a message; Shift+Enter adds a
line. The arrow inside the message box also sends. Messages can contain up to
280 characters; a small count appears near the arrow for the last 40 characters.
There are no attachments or links.

During a visit, the host room also includes its selected visitors. The Library
banner shows who is visiting. Visitors can read messages from their admission
onward until the visit ends. Hosts keep that conversation afterward; returning
visitors start a new chat window.

Close chat with Escape or a click outside; on phones, use **×** beside the
composer. Your draft stays while the page is open. If sending fails, retry without
retyping. The badge counts unread messages. Reading older messages keeps your place; **New messages ↓** returns
to the latest ones.

While chat is open, online friends with messages currently visible in the
history settle into places where their pictures can connect to the chat window.
They keep their places whenever possible, spreading across the row rather than
packing against the left edge. Scrolling also prepares nearby upcoming speakers
in the direction you're reading, using spare places without displacing visible
speakers. Your own profile control stays in place.

On desktop, speech tails appear only for messages in view, regardless of when
they were posted. Upcoming speakers get no tail until their message appears.
Tails open as the pictures finish moving; closing chat restores the usual order.
Reduced motion skips the animation. The compact phone layout keeps its
full-height conversation without speech tails.

You can remove your own messages; admins can remove anyone's. Chat keeps
recent conversation for up to seven days. Older messages may disappear sooner
as the room fills up; it is not a permanent archive.

## Install on your phone

Infuser can be installed from the browser menu or **Install Infuser** in the
profile menu. On iPhone/iPad, use **Share → Add to Home Screen**, then open that
icon. The installed website is named **Infuser**; **Infuser Play** remains the
native game launcher.

In your profile menu, turn **Chat notifications** on for each device you want.
Permission is requested only after that tap. On iOS/iPadOS, this requires the
Home Screen app (16.4 or later). If permission was denied, change it in the
browser/device settings before trying again. Turn the same switch off to stop
alerts on that device. Signing out unsubscribes that browser too.

Alerts work while the Library is closed. They say “Your group has a new message”
without including message text. They are quiet, share one notification slot,
and open chat when tapped. Arrival never navigates a window or exits fullscreen.
Delivery is best-effort and subject to the browser, OS notification settings and
network availability. No alerts for your own messages or groups you cannot access.
An active visit also allows alerts from its host room; queued alerts are checked
again before sending. Already delivered notifications cannot be recalled.
Offline, Infuser shows a reconnect page. Installing it does not download games or
saves for offline play. Website updates refresh the Library when it is idle;
they do not interrupt a game or discard a chat draft.
