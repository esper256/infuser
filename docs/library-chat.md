# Chat and phone notifications

Audience: players and admins.

Open **Chat** beside the online players at the top of the Library. Everyone in
your private group shares the room. Enter sends a message; Shift+Enter adds a
line. The arrow inside the message box also sends. Messages can contain up to
280 characters; a small count appears near the arrow for the last 40 characters.
Use the smiley beside the send arrow to pick emoji. Search matches parts of names
and keywords (two or more characters); categories, skin tones and frequently used
choices are also available. Selecting inserts at your cursor without sending.
Escape closes the picker first. The **Screenshots** tab attaches a saved game
screenshot; selecting one does not send it. Add a message, or send the image on
its own. Tap a thumbnail in chat to open the full image. Links remain plain text.

## Game screenshots

In fullscreen, swipe down starting just inside the game's top edge to reveal the
title bar. With a mouse, move the pointer to the top edge. Use the camera
beside **Fullscreen** to capture the game. In the browser's
sharing prompt, choose that game's tab. Infuser captures just the game area,
stops sharing immediately, and saves a compressed image to your current profile.
It does not record audio or post the image automatically. Capture currently needs
a desktop browser with tab cropping, such as Chrome or Edge. Unsupported browsers
show a message; you can still view and attach existing screenshots on your phone.

In chat, **Emoji → Screenshots** shows your latest ten captures. Older chat
attachments expire as you take new screenshots. The message text stays.

To react to a game, pick an emoji. Write a note beside it or use **Add
screenshot**. Tap the chosen emoji to change it while keeping your draft.
Each reaction can keep one screenshot for as long as it is attached, even
after ten newer captures. You can replace or remove it when editing the reaction.

Screenshots are private until you attach them. Attached images follow the same
group and visit access rules as their message or reaction. Guest-mode captures
belong to the signed-in profile; switching profiles gives you a separate collection.

## Conversations

During a visit, the host room also includes its selected visitors. The Library
banner shows who is visiting. Visitors can read messages from their admission
onward until the visit ends. Hosts keep that conversation afterward; returning
visitors start a new chat window.

Close chat with Escape or a click outside; on phones, use the floating **×** in the
upper-right corner. Your draft stays while the page is open. If sending fails, retry without
retyping. The badge counts unread messages. Reading older messages keeps your place; **New messages ↓** returns
to the latest ones.

While chat is open, online friends with messages currently visible in the
history settle into places where their pictures can connect to the chat window.
They keep their places whenever possible, spreading across the row rather than
packing against the left edge. Scrolling also prepares nearby upcoming speakers
in the direction you're reading, using spare places without displacing visible
speakers. Your own profile control stays in place.

A small copy of someone's profile picture sits on the last message they have
actually seen. It moves only forward, and it stays put across reloads and
their other devices. Several people who stopped on the same message overlap
in a short row. Point at a picture, or reach it with a screen reader, to hear
that person's name. Your own picture is not shown on this device: the mark is
how everyone else can see what you have read, and the line in front of you is
already where you are. A message counts only while some of it is inside the
open chat and this page is visible.

On desktop, speech tails appear only for messages in view, regardless of when
they were posted. Upcoming speakers get no tail until their message appears.
Tails open as the pictures finish moving; closing chat restores the usual order.
Reduced motion skips the animation. The compact phone layout keeps its
full-height conversation without speech tails.

You can remove your own messages; admins can remove anyone's. On a phone,
press and hold a message to open its actions. With a mouse, hover over its bubble
to reveal Delete; it becomes clickable after half a second. Keyboard users can
focus a message and press Shift+F10 for its actions. Chat keeps
recent conversation for up to seven days. Older messages may disappear sooner
as the room fills up; it is not a permanent archive.

## Install on your phone

Infuser can be installed from the browser menu or **Install Infuser** in the
profile menu. On iPhone/iPad, use **Share → Add to Home Screen**, then open that
icon. The installed website is named **Infuser**; **Infuser Play** remains the
native game launcher.

In your profile menu, turn **Group Chat notifications** on for each device you want.
Permission is requested only after that tap. On iOS/iPadOS, this requires the
Home Screen app (16.4 or later). If permission was denied, change it in the
browser/device settings before trying again. Turn the same switch off to stop
alerts on that device. Signing out unsubscribes that browser too.
On Android, the browser manages the system notification channel. Use the
Infuser profile menu to control Group Chat alerts on that device.

New subscriptions use **Sound: device default**. If you already had alerts on,
they stay quiet until you tap **Sound: quiet** in your profile menu to switch.
Tap the sound setting again to change it back. **Send test notification** sends
one alert to this device. It does not post to chat.

With device sound on, the first incoming alert can ring. A burst shares one
notification and stays quiet until five minutes pass without another message.
Alerts are quiet while you are reading chat on that device. Your phone controls
the tone, volume and Focus or Do Not Disturb settings.

Alerts work while the Library is closed. The lock screen shows a link to group
chat without message text. Tapping opens chat. Arrival preserves fullscreen.
Delivery is best-effort and subject to the browser, OS notification settings and
network availability. No alerts for your own messages or groups you cannot access.
An active visit also allows alerts from its host room; queued alerts are checked
again before sending. Already delivered notifications cannot be recalled.
Offline, Infuser shows a reconnect page. Installing it does not download games or
saves for offline play. Website updates refresh the Library when it is idle;
they do not interrupt a game or discard a chat draft.
