# Profiles

Audience: users and group admins.

If you have one profile, signing in opens your group directly. If you have
profiles in more than one group, Infuser asks which profile to open. Each card
shows your name, picture and group.

Use **Switch Profile** in your profile menu to choose another. Infuser reloads
the site into that group. Other open tabs and games keep their original profile.
Your names, pictures, uploaded games, permissions and saves are separate in each
group. Switching does not copy or merge them.

## Profile picture reminder

The first Library visit without a profile picture opens the picture picker once.
Close it to keep your initials, or choose a picture. Infuser remembers this on your
profile across browsers and devices; keeping initials does not cause repeat prompts.
You can always open **Change profile picture** from your own portrait in the top bar.
Profiles with pictures and read-only admin inspection do not show the reminder.

## Add another profile

A group admin can invite your existing Google email from **Admin tools**, just
as they would invite a new user. Infuser creates a separate profile in that
group. Your original profile stays unchanged. Inviting an existing member of
the same group edits that group's existing profile; it does not add a duplicate.

An operator can also use an existing Google email when creating a group and
choosing its first admin. Group-admin permission applies only to that profile.
Operator permission still belongs to the account.

## Notifications and Infuser Play

Chat notifications on a browser belong to one profile at a time. Switching turns
them off; enable them again in the new profile if you want them. A notification
opens the profile that enabled it after Infuser checks your sign-in.

When connecting Infuser Play, choose the profile you want that computer to use.
The confirmation names both the profile and group. Switching on the website
does not change a computer that is already connected; reconnect Infuser Play
when you want it to use another profile.

Profile selection uses browser site storage. If storage is blocked, a single
profile still works, but switching profiles requires allowing storage for Infuser.
